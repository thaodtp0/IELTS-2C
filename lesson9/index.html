Writing Studio — From Reading Vocabulary to Your Own Paragraph

Develop an IELTS-style B1+ paragraph using accurate vocabulary, explanations and examples.

Step 1 — Learn useful sentence patterns

Describe a process: Seeds can germinate when conditions are suitable.

Explain a cause: Poor drainage may cause plants to succumb to disease.

Express caution: It would be an exaggeration to claim that every plant grows quickly.

Interpret evidence: A sudden drop in rainfall may be indicative of a wider environmental problem.

Step 2 — Upgrade your sentences

Rewrite each simple idea using the suggested vocabulary. Compare with a model after making an attempt.

Step 3 — Plan your paragraph

Choose one prompt and answer the planning questions in English.

Step 4 — Write 110–150 words

Include a topic sentence, a reason, an example and a concluding sentence. Aim to use at least four relevant target expressions naturally.
